# The World in One Day — Saturday, 21 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, for the
make.fun competition `news-image-20261121`.

## Stories (all happened or first reported 21 November 2026)

1. **NATO chief flies to Warsaw after missiles land in Poland** — Mark Rutte
   visits a day after Russian missiles came down on NATO soil (AP; Reuters).
2. **BepiColombo slips into orbit around Mercury** — after eight years and
   6 billion miles, the ESA–JAXA probe fires its thrusters at 19:24 CET
   (ESA; Sky & Telescope).
3. **Saudi defences down three Houthi missiles over Riyadh** — air-raid
   alerts for a third night in a row; no damage or casualties (Reuters).
4. **Indonesia and EU sign landmark trade pact** — 98% of bilateral trade
   worth $30bn a year liberalised after nearly a decade of talks (ANTARA).
5. **Takaichi held no Taiwan talks at APEC** — Asahi reports Japan's PM met
   no Taiwanese envoy in Shenzhen, despite two handshakes with Xi (Asahi).
6. **Las Vegas lights up for F1 night race** — fifty laps under the Strip
   lights, with Kimi Antonelli already crowned world champion (Formula 1).
7. **NSW lets coalmine lift output by a third** — special legislation lets
   the Mount Pleasant mine raise production to 16.5m tonnes a year (NSW).
8. **SCO forum opens dialogue in Delhi** — the first civilisational forum
   holds its sessions, with scholars from ten member states (The Hindu).

## Files

- `index.html` — the newspaper front-page design.
- `generate.mjs` — renders `index.html` to `entry.png` (1024×1024) with the
  local Chromium through Playwright. Run: `node generate.mjs`.
- `entry.png` — the rendered image (≤1 MiB, 1024×1024).
- `main.py` — the entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
